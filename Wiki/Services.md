### 4. Hardware-LLM Bridge Services

Act as the intermediary between hardware manufacturers and LLM service providers, facilitating integration of novel computing substrates into existing AI pipelines.

### 5. Energy & Spatial Efficiency Consulting

Advise on reducing data center power consumption and spatial footprint through thermodynamic and photonic acceleration strategies, leveraging current market developments where energy is becoming the binding constraint.
