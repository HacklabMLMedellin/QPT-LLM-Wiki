# Strategic Project Scoring Framework & Roadmap Evaluation System

## 1. Executive Summary & Scoring Philosophy

To systematically quantify project progress, operational velocity, and roadmap maturity across diverse technical and institutional milestones, this framework establishes a **Tiered Leveling & Unit Value Scoring System**.

Because absolute counts of unbounded achievements (e.g., academic publications, keynote invitations, intellectual property filings) cannot be constrained by fixed upper bounds, the system evaluates success across two distinct operational paradigms:

1. **Deterministic Foundations & Infrastructure:** Evaluated using a normalized **Completion Scale** ($C \in [0.0, 1.0]$) applied to established Base Point Values.
2. **Recurring Strategic Milestones:** Evaluated using **Unit Values** where cumulative points scale directly per verified instance, reflecting high-impact breakthroughs, institutional validation, and global leadership.

## 3. Mathematical Evaluation & Target Formula

To establish the total **QPT Soft Success Score ($S$)** at any evaluation checkpoint, compute the aggregate sum across foundational completion vectors and recurring milestone products:

$$S = S_{\text{Foundations}} + S_{\text{Hardware}} + \sum_{i=1}^{N_P} (P_i \times 300) + \sum_{j=1}^{N_A} (A_j \times 150) + \sum_{k=1}^{N_T} (T_k \times 500) + \sum_{l=1}^{N_G} (G_l \times 600) + \sum_{m=1}^{N_C} (C_m \times 250)$$

Where:

* $S_{\text{Foundations}} = \sum (C_{\text{foundations}} \times B_{\text{foundations}})$
* $S_{\text{Hardware}} = \sum (C_{\text{hardware}} \times B_{\text{hardware}})$
* $P$ = Peer-Reviewed Publications
* $A$ = Released Hybrid Algorithms
* $T$ = Granted Patents
* $G$ = Awarded Grants
* $C$ = International Keynotes & Conference Presentations

## 4. Strategic Valuation Guidance

* **Baseline Momentum (100–300 pts):** Brand, digital presence, and foundational physical infrastructure establish operational readiness and provide initial scoring momentum.
* **Strategic High-Tier Spikes (500–2,500 pts):** Non-classical computing hardware acquisitions, major financial grant unlocks, and granted intellectual property deliver major trajectory shifts in the project's cumulative value score.
