# Product

## Vision

MP-LLM exists to answer a single research question honestly:

> *Which parts of a language model actually benefit from non-classical computing, and by how much?*


We build and doing real scince, runnable implementations, papers, code bases and simulations — for Classical, Quantum, Photonic, and Thermodynamic paradigms. Every timing result distinguishes between wall-clock simulation time and theoretical hardware-adjusted time. Every paradigm selection comes with a human-readable rationale. The goal is to have an advantage in the AI race when the hardware boom explodes, advising current AI giants and producing better language models, while helping solve global issues like energy consumption and data center spatial constraints.
